TargumaFounder · AI-native publishing · 2026

Books that cross languages.

The Kindle store doesn't sell books in Amharic, a language read by tens of millions of people. So I built a publisher for the languages big stores leave out. AI drafts the translation, the author reviews it line by line beside the original, and nothing ships without their consent. Readers get both editions side by side, in days instead of years.

  • AI translation pipeline that drafts a whole book and aligns it paragraph by paragraph for review
  • Publishing rail that turns one manuscript into an ebook and a print-on-demand paperback
  • Web store and Flutter apps for iOS and Android, with Stripe and in-app purchases
  • Agent Abay, an AI teammate that writes code and reviews every pull request

How I build

One builder. A team's output.

AI lets one person build what used to take a whole team. This is the workflow I use every day and teach in Udacity's Introduction to AI-SDLC. Agents do the typing. I do the judgment.

01

Frame the problem

Start from a real person with a real problem. Write the spec, the success metric and the checks that tell me when the AI is good enough.

spec.md
02

Build with agents

Claude, Cursor and Copilot write the first drafts. I own the architecture, read every diff and decide what ships.

claude · cursor · copilot
03

Review like a team

An AI reviewer checks every pull request and builds test binaries before a human merges. Quality doesn't depend on headcount.

agent-abay → PR review
04

Ship to every screen

Web, iOS and Android, with payments, analytics and store releases done properly. A product, not a demo.

About

A builder at heart. AI is the superpower.

I'm Kidus, a software engineer in Oakland. I studied computer science at UC Santa Cruz, then spent seven years shipping mobile apps at scale: news, finance, lending and health.

Then AI changed the math. With good taste and good agents, one person can now design, build and ship what used to need a team. I use that leverage to build for people the big platforms overlook, starting with my own community: Amharic readers, Ethiopian Orthodox families and the diaspora.

Away from the keyboard I read a lot of fiction and stay curious about almost everything, which is usually how the next product starts.
